Rokas played at school, stopped for fifteen years, and came back through Lichess during evening shifts. He joined Rimantas's Saturday group at a Kaunas club six months ago. He plays one weekend rapid tournament a month (results on chess-results.com) and many 10+0 games on Lichess.

## Goals

- Stop losing in the first ten moves to traps he "has seen before".
- Know one solid answer to each common first move, nothing more.
- Reach 1500 Lichess rapid and play his first FIDE-rated tournament by the end of the season.
- Show up to Saturday sessions having done the homework Rimantas assigned.

## Frustrations

- Long opening videos and books: he forgets everything by the next game.
- Apps that throw 20-move lines at him on day one.
- English-only chess terms ("fianchetto", "tempo") without a Lithuanian explanation.
- Small touch targets on the phone; dragging pieces on a bus.
- Not knowing whether he is "done" for today.

## Training habits

- 10-15 minutes a day on the phone, usually on a break or on the bus, often with patchy connectivity.
- Does the daily review if a notification reminds him; skips it otherwise.
- Prefers tapping a piece then a square (tap-tap) over dragging.
- Reads coach notes, rarely writes his own; may ask a question in the group thread.

## What success means

- The app tells him exactly what is due today and that it takes about 10 minutes.
- He gets through a week of reviews with a visible streak.
- In real games he stays "in book" for the first 6-8 moves, and when he deviates he sees which move he forgot.
- Rimantas can see he did the homework without Rokas having to report it.
